Phone as a Private Cloud AI Server (via Tailscale, free)

Turn your phone into a private on-device AI server accessible from your laptop or iPad over your encrypted Tailscale tailnet—without exposing port 8080 to public Wi-Fi:

1. Install Tailscale on your phone and laptop.

2. Start Nullcloud bound to your Tailscale IP: TAILSCALE=1 bash ~/nullcloud/termux/start.sh

3. In the laptop Browser Extension or Web UI, set the Server URL to your phone's Tailscale address (http://100.x.y.z:8080).
